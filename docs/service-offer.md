# Upwork Project Catalog drafts

## Single AI PR Evidence Review

**Buyer-facing title** — I will review your AI-generated pull request and verify its test evidence

**Description** — Get an engineer-reviewed report for one GitHub pull request before you decide whether to merge. I compare the change to your acceptance criteria, review its test coverage and check whether available CI and execution evidence applies to the exact revision. The report includes prioritized findings, the evidence behind them and any remaining checks you need to make.

| Tier | Proposed asking price | Scope | Delivery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | US$99 | One PR, up to 300 changed source lines and 10 reviewed source files | Two business days after complete intake |
| Standard | US$149 | One PR, up to 600 changed source lines and 20 reviewed source files, with one follow-up on a corrected claim or revised PR within the original scope | Three business days after complete intake |

**Buyer requirements** — Provide the PR or a redacted patch, base and head SHA, requirements, relevant check URLs or redacted logs, and any known gaps. Confirm which repository and material may be reviewed. A new test run needs an explicitly authorized isolated environment and agreed side effects. Private access, if needed, uses least-privilege access through the agreed repository interface. Share customer data only through agreed, authorized channels and remove secrets from all materials.

**Deliverables** — A Markdown report with the reviewed revision and scope, findings with file references, evidence for CI and test claims, relevant limitations, and practical verification steps. The service ends with the report and the stated follow-up. Code changes, repository-wide review, production access, penetration tests and merge operations are outside the offer. If evidence is incomplete or stale, the report records what can be verified and what remains open.

## AI Agent Acceptance Review

**Buyer-facing title** — I will evaluate your AI agent against observable acceptance criteria

**Description** — Get an engineer-reviewed evaluation for one agent workflow. I examine defined tasks and outcomes, assess available run evidence and identify failures, gaps and the cases that need a new run. Each case has an expected behavior, an outcome and a reference to supporting evidence.

| Tier | Proposed asking price | Scope | Delivery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | US$199 | One workflow, up to four cases evaluated from supplied logs | Three business days after complete intake |
| Standard | US$299 | One workflow, up to six cases in total, including up to two approved runs in an isolated environment | Five business days after complete intake |

**Buyer requirements** — Provide the workflow, agent/version, expected results, redacted logs or synthetic fixtures and any relevant constraints. For live runs, define the permitted tool actions, sandbox and expected state changes. Explicitly authorized cases can assess retry, recovery or output handling using controlled inputs and read-only or reversible effects.

**Deliverables** — A Markdown report with case-level PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED or NOT_RUN, evidence links or redacted excerpts, and prioritized remediation. Production access, unrestricted tool runs and irreversible actions are outside the offer.

## Draft economics and rollout

Price and turnaround are proposals for owner approval. Project Catalog reviews offers before publication and supports fixed-price tiers and media. At an illustrative 15% contract fee, US$99 yields US$84.15 before withdrawal, processing or taxes. Use the actual fee shown per contract, currently 0%–15%, and measure intake, review and support time per order. Direct to Local Bank is US$0.99 where available. Taiwan-specific account methods require confirmation in the owner account.
