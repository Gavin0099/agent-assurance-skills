Visible offers establish alternatives for workflow automation, recurring PR review and human advice. They suggest three hypotheses to test in buyer interviews or a paid pilot. Buyers may pay for an auditable snapshot after discovering that checks refer to an earlier revision, for a short report focused on a release decision, or for acceptance evidence around silent agent failures. Ask which incident brought them to the offer, which evidence they had to assemble manually, and whether a specific report would have changed their decision. Track volunteered reasons, verified usage and paid commitments separately.

The roadmap's US$19 one-time Pro candidate and US$99 single-PR pilot are price hypotheses. The Pro candidate competes with a lower-priced workflow pack and recurring tools. Its purchase case depends on evidence capture, deterministic verification and reusable output. A fixed-scope review covers one change and can be shorter than the repository audits above.

MCP Market's seller landing page shows illustrative price, order and revenue numbers without independently verifiable offer records. MintSkills advertises a beta/catalog preview. Neither establishes realized sales. Registry downloads, rank and GitHub stars can guide discovery. The [validation log](commercial-validation.md) records direct evidence used for product decisions.
